\section{Chess}


Being one of the oldest board games, chess has a large history and is actively played even these days. Its predecessor is called \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaturanga}{Chaturanga}, and it originated from India, in the 6th century. The name is said to come either from ``having four limbs'', or ``army'', the four parts of the Indian army being elephants, chariots, horsemen and foot soldiers, predecessors of pieces bishop, rook, knight and pawn. Some say it was initially played with dice. The word "Shāh!" means King in Persian, while ``Shāh Māt!'' means the king is helpless. These sounds are still in use in many languages for saying check and checkmate (for example: in Hungarian, we say Sakk and Sakk-Matt). The game is described in the 13th-century Spanish manuscript \textit{Libro de los juegos}, the oldest document on European table games.

Over the years different chess boards have evolved. One of, if not the oldest one, is \href{http://history.chess.free.fr/afrasiab.htm}{The Afrasiab Chessmen}. It is made of ivory and consists of 7 pieces. It is dated from the 6th to 8th century. \href{https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/galleries/medieval-europe}{The Lewis Chessmen} were found on the Isle of Lewis, Scotland, dating to 1150–1175. Made from walrus ivory and whales' teeth, it's believed that they belonged to a merchant traveling from Norway to Ireland. The \href{https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/202506}{Russian Chess set
from Kholmogory} is said to come from the late 18th–early 19th century. Interestingly all pieces are of the same color, although they depict opposing armies, Russian Christians versus Muslims. \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staunton_chess_set}{The Staunton chess set} is the current standard approved by FIDE, and most of the chess sets we see on the market today resemble its pieces.

Some of the important moments in modern chess history are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Chess_Championship_1886}{The first official World Chess Championship, 1886}
    \item \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIDE}{Foundation of World Chess Federation (FIDE), 1924}
    \item \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Chess_Club}{Internet Chess Club, the beginning of online chess, 1992}
    \item \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_Blue_versus_Garry_Kasparov}{Deep Blue versus Garry Kasparov, 1997}
    \item \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasparov_versus_the_World}{Kasparov versus the world, 1999}
\end{itemize}

The fact that chess became so popular and has lived on for so long is a clear indicator of its ingenuity. For me, chess was always the best board game. It is impossible to see the result of a move and to think ahead of all the moves, since the number of possibilities exponentially grows with each move, and you cannot predict the enemy. However, I think it is safe to say that there is no luck in chess, and this is what makes it so cool for me. No luck, just the limitation of our minds.


\section{Digital Image Processing}

\href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_image_processing}{Digital image processing is the use of a digital computer to process digital images through an algorithm}. Images can be thought of as arrays of pixels. Based on how we perceive the image, pixels can be represented differently. A grayscale image's pixels have intensity values ranging from 0 to 255, where 0 is black and 255 is white. A color image's pixels are three-channeled, green-red-blue, each channel having a value from 0 to 255. A mixture of these colors achieves all possible colors and gives us a granularity that is more than enough for almost all purposes.

Digital Image Processing is a wide term and its concrete applications include classification, feature extraction, and pattern recognition. Many of the terms used in this domain are similar or overlay, and somewhat ambiguous. A powerful technique to process digital images is through filtering, which is mostly done through convolution. Filters can be used to make images blurrier or crisper, to detect edges. Affine transformations allow us to reflect, scale, rotate, and shear the original image. Morphological operations such as dilation, erosion, opening and closing are suitable for denoising images. 

The processed images are often passed on for classification algorithms. \href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_classification}{In statistics, classification is the problem of identifying which of a set of categories (sub-populations) an observation (or observations) belongs to.}. In practice, classification can be single or multiclass, regression or classification. A multitude of algorithms is developed for this task, such as Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes Classifier, The Perceptron Algorithm, Support Vector Machine, Artificial Neural Networks, Decision Trees, and K Nearest Neighbors. Applications are all over the world, and we see a constant rise in the popularity of what outsiders refer to as AI.
